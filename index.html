This Privacy Policy explains what information ITI CBT Practice ("the App") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By creating an account or using the App, you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

InformationWhy we collect it
Name and email address (from Google Sign-In or the email you register with)To create and identify your student account and to show your name in the App.
Unique account ID (created by Firebase Authentication)To keep your results and progress linked to your account.

We do not ask for, and the App does not collect, your Aadhaar number, phone number, bank or card details, precise location, contacts, or photos.

2. How we use information

  • To run the App: sign you in, show questions, save and review your tests.
  • To improve the question bank, for example by reviewing reported questions.
  • To show anonymous average scores for each mock test. Individual students are never named or shown to others.
  • To show advertisements and measure how they perform (see section 5).
  • To keep the App secure and prevent misuse.

3. Where data is stored

Your account and test data are stored in Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore). Firebase is provided by Google LLC. Some questions and settings may also be kept on your device so the App works offline or saves time.

4. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with:

  • Google (Firebase): as the technical service provider that stores the data for us.
  • Google AdMob (advertising partner): to show ads in the App. See section 5.
  • Authorities: if required by law.

5. Advertising

The App shows advertisements provided by Google AdMob. AdMob and its partners may use your device's advertising identifier and other device information to show ads and measure their performance. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

Where required by law, the App asks for your consent before showing personalised ads, using Google's consent form. If you do not consent, the App shows non-personalised ads.

Ads are not shown during timed exams, on the exam instructions screen, or on the result screen. The App does not sell your personal information.

To learn how Google uses data from apps and sites that use its services, see: How Google uses data from sites and apps that use our services.

If we later offer a paid plan that removes ads, this policy will be updated before that change takes effect.

6. Children and students

The App is designed for students of Industrial Training Institutes, some of whom may be under 18. If you are under 18, please use the App with the knowledge of a parent, guardian or instructor. If you believe a child has given us information without permission, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Data retention and deletion

We keep your account and results while your account is active. You can ask us to delete your account and all related data by writing to the contact email above. We will delete it within 30 days, unless we must keep certain records by law.

8. Your rights

You can ask us to view, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you. You can also stop using the App at any time and log out from the App menu.

9. Security

We use Google's security tools, access rules that allow each student to see only their own results, and encrypted connections (HTTPS). No system is completely secure, so please keep your password private.
